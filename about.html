About

I grew up in Los Angeles, CA where I found my passion for baseball. I played the game I love for 20 years and my career ended at St. Norbert College in May 2021. I stepped away from the game for a few years, but I have found myself coming back to it. I'm currently coaching kids in the South Bay of Los Angeles and I will be helping coach the Philippines Baseball Group's high school team this summer.

Outside of my work life, I try stay active. I enjoy playing basketball, golf, tennis, pickleball and ping-pong. I challenge any one to try and beat me at a game of ping-pong. I also enjoy woodworking and working on my car.

At this stage of life, I'm trying to figure out what I want to do in the long run. Hopefully, it can be a mixture of software development and baseball. You can stay up to date with my journey on this website!
